# ChannelHub Reporting Service Design Blueprint

## Purpose

Mendefinisikan layanan pengolahan laporan bisnis.

---

# Service Responsibility

```
Reporting Service
        |
Data Aggregation
        |
Business Insight
```

---

# Core Capability

Reporting Service menangani:

- Operational report.
- Reservation report.
- Revenue report.
- Synchronization report.

---

# Data Principle

Reporting dapat menggunakan:

- Aggregated data.
- Historical record.
- Analytics storage.

---

# Goal

Menyediakan informasi bisnis untuk pengambilan keputusan.
