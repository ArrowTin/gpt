# ChannelHub Staff Level Workflow

## Purpose

Mendefinisikan tingkatan staff dan batas tanggung jawab operasional agar akses tetap aman dan mudah dikontrol.

---

# 1. Staff Hierarchy

```
Level 4 - Owner / Executive
Level 3 - Administrator
Level 2 - Manager
Level 1 - Operational Staff
```

---

# 2. Level 4 Executive

Akses:

- Business dashboard.
- Financial report.
- Subscription management.
- Approval critical action.

---

# 3. Level 3 Administrator

Akses:

- User management.
- Configuration.
- Property setup.
- Permission assignment sesuai batas.

---

# 4. Level 2 Manager

Akses:

- Reservation monitoring.
- Inventory control.
- Staff supervision.
- Operational approval.

---

# 5. Level 1 Operational Staff

Akses:

- Daily operation.
- Reservation handling.
- Customer interaction.

Tidak memiliki akses konfigurasi kritikal.

---

# 6. Dynamic Permission

Level bukan sumber akses final.

Final decision:

```
Role
+
Permission
+
Policy
+
Feature Entitlement
```
