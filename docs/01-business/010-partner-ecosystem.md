# ChannelHub Partner Ecosystem

## Purpose

Dokumen ini mendefinisikan ekosistem partner ChannelHub, hubungan antar pihak, dan peluang bisnis yang dapat dikembangkan melalui platform.

---

# 1. Partner Ecosystem Overview

ChannelHub dibangun sebagai platform yang menghubungkan berbagai pihak:

```
                ChannelHub
                    |
    --------------------------------
    |              |                |
   OTA       Technology       Hospitality
 Partners      Partners        Partners
```

---

# 2. OTA Partner

OTA partner menyediakan channel distribusi untuk properti.

Contoh:

- Booking.com.
- Agoda.
- Traveloka.
- Airbnb.
- Expedia.
- Tiket.com.

Peran:

- Marketplace distribution.
- Reservation source.
- Customer acquisition.

Hubungan bisnis:

- API integration.
- Partnership agreement.
- Revenue sharing jika tersedia.

---

# 3. Technology Partner

Partner teknologi mendukung operasional ChannelHub.

Contoh:

- Payment provider.
- Cloud provider.
- Monitoring provider.
- Communication provider.

Peran:

- Infrastruktur.
- Pembayaran.
- Notifikasi.
- Reliability.

---

# 4. Hospitality Partner

Partner yang berada di industri hospitality.

Contoh:

- Hotel.
- Villa.
- Homestay.
- Property management company.

Peran:

- Pengguna utama platform.
- Penyedia inventory.
- Pengembang ecosystem.

---

# 5. Reseller / Agency Partner

ChannelHub dapat memiliki partner penjualan.

Peran:

- Mendapatkan subscriber baru.
- Membantu onboarding.
- Memberikan layanan lokal.

Model bisnis:

- Commission.
- Referral fee.
- Partnership plan.

---

# 6. Marketplace Ecosystem

Jika ChannelHub memiliki marketplace sendiri:

Partner dapat berpartisipasi melalui:

- Featured listing.
- Promotion placement.
- Campaign partnership.

---

# 7. Partner Management System

Sistem membutuhkan modul:

```
partner-service

- Partner Profile
- Contract Management
- Commission Rule
- Revenue Report
- Partner Settlement
```

---

# 8. Partner Governance

Setiap partner harus memiliki:

- Status.
- Agreement.
- Access scope.
- API credential management.
- Audit history.

---

# 9. Business Principle

Ekosistem partner harus memberikan nilai dua arah.

```
More Partners
      |
      v
More Capability
      |
      v
More Subscriber Value
      |
      v
More Revenue Opportunity
```

---

# 10. Technical Principle

Partner integration harus:

- Modular.
- Secure.
- Observable.
- Independently maintainable.

Credential partner tidak boleh disimpan sembarangan dan harus menggunakan secure credential management.
